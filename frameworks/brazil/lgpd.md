## Overview

The LGPD is Brazil's comprehensive personal data protection law, modeled closely on the European Union's General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the OECD Privacy Guidelines. It governs the processing (*tratamento*) of personal data (*dados pessoais*) of individuals located in Brazil, regardless of where the processing organization is headquartered.

**Extraterritorial reach:** Like the GDPR, LGPD applies to any entity that:
- Processes personal data in Brazil (*território nacional*)
- Offers goods or services to individuals in Brazil
- Collects data from individuals located in Brazil

## 10 Core Principles (Art. 6)

| # | Principle (*Princípio*) | Description |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Purpose** (*Finalidade*) | Processing must have a legitimate, specific, explicit purpose communicated to the data subject |
| 2 | **Adequacy** (*Adequação*) | Processing must be compatible with the stated purpose |
| 3 | **Necessity** (*Necessidade*) | Only minimum data necessary for the purpose may be processed |
| 4 | **Free Access** (*Livre Acesso*) | Data subjects must have easy, free access to their data and how it is processed |
| 5 | **Data Quality** (*Qualidade dos Dados*) | Data must be accurate, clear, relevant, and up to date |
| 6 | **Transparency** (*Transparência*) | Clear, accessible information about processing must be available |
| 7 | **Security** (*Segurança*) | Technical and administrative measures must protect data from unauthorized access |
| 8 | **Prevention** (*Prevenção*) | Measures must be taken to prevent harm from processing |
| 9 | **Non-Discrimination** (*Não Discriminação*) | Processing must not enable unlawful or abusive discrimination |
| 10 | **Accountability** (*Responsabilização e Prestação de Contas*) | Controllers must demonstrate compliance with the LGPD |

## Legal Bases for Processing (Art. 7 — General Data)

Processing of personal data is only lawful when based on one of the following:

1. **Consent** (*Consentimento*) — Free, informed, unambiguous consent for a specific purpose
2. **Legal or regulatory compliance** (*Cumprimento de obrigação legal ou regulatória*)
3. **Public policy execution** (*Execução de políticas públicas*)
4. **Research** (*Estudos por órgão de pesquisa*) — Anonymization preferred
5. **Contract performance** (*Execução de contrato ou procedimentos preliminares*)
6. **Exercise of rights** (*Exercício regular de direitos*)
7. **Legitimate interests** (*Interesses legítimos*) — Subject to balancing test
8. **Credit protection** (*Proteção do crédito*)
9. **Life protection** (*Tutela da saúde / vida*)

### Legal Bases for Sensitive Data (Art. 11)

Sensitive data (*dados pessoais sensíveis*) — including race/ethnicity, religion, political opinion, union membership, health, sex life, biometric, and genetic data — requires one of:

- Explicit consent for a specific purpose
- Legal/regulatory obligation
- Anonymized data for research
- Life/physical integrity protection
- Fraud prevention or data subject safety
- Healthcare services (by health professionals under confidentiality)
- ANPD-authorized public interest purposes

## Data Subject Rights (Art. 17–22)

Data subjects (*titulares*) in Brazil have the following rights:

| Right | Description | Response Timeframe |
|---|---|---|
| Confirmation of processing | Confirm whether their data is processed | Immediate / expedited format |
| Access | Receive a copy of data held | 15 days from request |
| Correction | Correct inaccurate, incomplete, or outdated data | — |
| Anonymization / Blocking / Deletion | For data processed without legal basis | — |
| Portability | Transfer data to another service provider (subject to ANPD regulation) | — |
| Deletion of consented data | Delete data processed with consent | — |
| Information on sharing | Know which public/private entities data was shared with | — |
| Right to refuse / withdraw consent | Revoke consent at any time | — |
| Review of automated decisions | Request human review of decisions made solely by automated processing (Art. 20) | — |

## DPO (Encarregado) Requirements (Art. 37–39)

- Controllers (*controladores*) must appoint a **Data Protection Officer** (*Encarregado de Proteção de Dados*)
- The DPO may be an individual or a legal entity
- Identity and contact details must be publicly available (website, privacy notice)
- Responsibilities: receive data subject complaints, communicate with ANPD, advise employees, carry out other duties set by the controller

> **Note:** ANPD Resolution CD/ANPD No. 2/2022 creates a simplified compliance track for SMBs, startups, and low-risk processing organizations that may reduce some DPO obligations.

## International Data Transfers (Art. 33–36)

International transfers (*transferências internacionais de dados pessoais*) are permitted only when:

| Mechanism | Description |
|---|---|
| **Adequate country** | Destination country has equivalent data protection level (ANPD maintains adequacy list) |
| **Standard contractual clauses** | ANPD-approved SCCs between controller and foreign recipient |
| **Binding corporate rules** | Approved BCRs for intragroup transfers |
| **Specific consent** | Explicit, specific consent from the data subject |
| **Treaty / international legal cooperation** | Under applicable international agreements |
| **Vital interests** | Protection of life or physical integrity |
| **Public registry data** | Transfer of data from public registers |

> **BYOC relevance:** When a customer deploys HailBytes in their own cloud account in Brazil, training data and logs never leave the customer's environment — eliminating international transfer concerns entirely.

## ANPD — National Data Protection Authority

The **Autoridade Nacional de Proteção de Dados (ANPD)** was formally established in 2020. Key powers:

- Issue binding regulations and guidelines
- Conduct investigations (*fiscalização*) and audits
- Apply administrative sanctions
- Approve adequacy decisions and BCRs
- Promote public awareness

ANPD has prioritized enforcement in financial services, health, and large-scale consumer data processing.

## Penalties and Sanctions (Art. 52–54)

| Sanction | Maximum |
|---|---|
| Warning (*advertência*) with a remediation deadline | — |
| Simple fine (*multa simples*) | 2% of revenue in Brazil in the prior fiscal year (legal entity, group, or conglomerate), up to **R$ 50,000,000** per infraction |
| Daily fine (*multa diária*) | Up to R$ 50,000,000 per day |
| Public disclosure of violation | — |
| Blocking of personal data | Until remediation |
| Deletion of personal data | Affected data |
| Partial suspension of database operations | Up to 6 months, extendable |
| Prohibition on personal data processing activities | Partial or total |

Aggravating factors include: recidivism, good faith of the agent, the degree of harm, and cooperation with ANPD.

## LGPD vs. GDPR — Key Comparisons

| Dimension | LGPD (Brazil) | GDPR (EU) |
|---|---|---|
| **Scope** | Any processing in/for Brazil | Any processing in/for EU |
| **Legal bases** | 10 (Art. 7) | 6 (Art. 6) |
| **Sensitive data** | Art. 11 | Art. 9 |
| **DPO** | Required for controllers | Required based on risk/scale |
| **Breach notification** | "Reasonable timeframe" per ANPD guidance (typically 2 business days awareness + full report) | 72 hours to supervisory authority |
| **Max fine** | 2% Brazil revenue / R$50M per infraction | 4% global turnover / €20M |
| **Adequacy** | ANPD maintains its own adequacy list | European Commission maintains EU list |
| **Data subject rights** | Broadly similar; portability pending ANPD regulation | Fully operative |
| **Enforcement body** | ANPD (single national authority) | 27 national DPAs (one per EU member) |

## Key Takeaways for Enterprise Buyers

1. **LGPD has real teeth** — sanctions have been issued since 2022; financial services and health are priority sectors
2. **DPO is mandatory** — your vendor must have a publicly identified Encarregado
3. **International transfers require a legal mechanism** — BYOC deployment eliminates this risk for training/security data
4. **Consent is not the only legal basis** — legitimate interest and contractual necessity are commonly used in B2B contexts
5. **ANPD is maturing fast** — expect more prescriptive guidance on security measures and breach notification in 2024–2025
6. **Map to GDPR** — if you are GDPR-compliant, LGPD alignment is achievable with targeted gap analysis
